# Archived: the original Astra collection

These files come from the earlier **Astra Oneshots** collection, from before the project became Oneshot Arcade. They describe the old per-port launcher (`outputs/arcade/serve.mjs`, ports 4173–4187) and the eight GPT-6 Astra builds as they shipped.

Paths such as `outputs/<game>/` in these documents now live at `builds/gpt-6-astra-high/<game-id>/`. The old launcher has been replaced by the arcade dashboard (`npm start`). They are kept unchanged as historical evidence.
